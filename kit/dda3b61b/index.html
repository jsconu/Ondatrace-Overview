Your PM Agent Starter Kit

Three AI skills that read your email, chat and project tools on a schedule and keep you ahead of the project. Thanks for joining.

Download the kit (ZIP)

Open START-HERE.md inside for install steps. I've also emailed you this link so you can find it later.

What's inside

  1. Build the Project MemoryOne short, sourced file with a project's people, decisions, dates and open questions, so every new chat starts from the same context. Refreshes weekly.
  2. Track My PromisesFinds what you said you'd do and what others owe you, across email, chat and meeting transcripts. Flags what's overdue every workday morning.
  3. Sweep the WeekEvery Friday, a short list of what actually moved on your project, what contradicts something said earlier, and what never happened.

Start here

Claude: upload each of the three skill ZIPs inside the kit under Settings > Capabilities > Skills, then connect the tools you want them to read.

ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot: open a skill's SKILL.md in the source folder, copy the text and paste it into a chat.

First run: pick one real project and say "Build the project memory for <project>." The other two skills build on it.
