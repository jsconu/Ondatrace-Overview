Change scoping and impact assessment

Work out how big the change is before you commit to a plan. Answer 24 questions across people, process and technology. Each is scored 1 to 5, with a plain description of what each end means.

  • Unknown and N/A answers stay visible until someone resolves them.
  • Category scores show the highest single score next to the average.
  • Each affected group is listed once along with its headcount assumptions.

Change inventory and delivery actions

Record what changes and how it affects each role group, team and enterprise function. Link communications, training and other actions to the impacts they cover.

  • One shared action can cover several impacts and is counted once.
  • An impact closes only with evidence or an approved exception.
  • Revising a change flags the impacts and actions to review.
