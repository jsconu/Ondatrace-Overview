Guide

Use Ondatrace in Claude

Connect Ondatrace to Claude to see what needs your attention, check a project and act on a finding without leaving the conversation. Questions: ondatrace@ondatrace.com.

What it does

Ondatrace reads the project tools, email, chat and meetings your organization has connected, and keeps findings about your projects with the evidence behind each one. The Claude connector lets Claude read those saved findings for you and shows them in an Ondatrace card inside the conversation.

  • Claude acts as you, in one organization, and sees only what you can see in Ondatrace.
  • Claude reads what Ondatrace has already saved. Asking a question doesn't start a new scan of your tools.
  • Nothing is sent, scheduled or approved without you pressing a button in the card.

Before you start

  • An Ondatrace account that belongs to an organization. If you don't have one, contact us.
  • A Claude plan that lets you add custom connectors. On a team plan, your Claude administrator may need to add the connector first.
  • At least one source connected in Ondatrace, so there is something to read.

Connect

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Ondatrace and enter the address https://app.ondatrace.com/mcp.
  3. Choose Connect. Claude opens an Ondatrace page in your browser. Sign in if asked.
  4. Pick the organization, choose what Claude may do (see below) and choose Connect.
  5. Back in Claude, start a new chat and ask: Open Ondatrace.

What Claude may do

You choose this on the Ondatrace approval page. Your administrator can limit which options you are offered.

PermissionWhat it allows
ReadSee findings, projects, decisions, portfolio reviews, source health and your own support cases. Always included.
SuggestAdd a note, rate a finding, propose a project change, set a date to look again, ask a teammate in Ondatrace to check a date, prepare a portfolio review draft and report an issue to support. A proposal changes nothing until a person approves it.
DecideUse the buttons in the card to approve or decline a proposed change, save a new target date, send a follow-up message or schedule a meeting. These are your clicks. Claude can't press them for you.

Acting on a finding in the card

Open a finding to see the suggested next steps. Depending on the finding and the tools you've connected, the card can:

  • draft a follow-up message and send it from your email or chat account once you press Send
  • fill in a calendar invite for you to finish in your own calendar
  • schedule a Zoom or Webex meeting once you press Schedule
  • check whether you can edit a Smartsheet plan, and who can if you can't
  • change a target date in Ondatrace, with a preview first. Your other tools aren't changed.

You see the recipient and the full text before anything goes out. Sending, scheduling and saving a date need the Decide permission.

Notes from your other Claude connectors

If you ask it to, Claude can save a short note in Ondatrace about something it found in another tool you've connected to Claude. The note is private to you, marked unverified, never shown to your team and deleted after 30 days. You can ask Claude to list or delete your notes at any time. Claude doesn't do this unless you ask.

Troubleshooting

What you seeWhat to do
Claude says to sign in to Ondatrace firstOpen the Ondatrace connector in Claude and choose Connect, then finish the approval page.
Claude can read but can't save a note or proposalThe connection is read-only. In Claude choose Disconnect, then Connect, and tick Suggest on the approval page. Reconnect alone may keep read-only access. If Suggest isn't offered, ask your Ondatrace administrator.
The permission checkboxes never appearRemove the Ondatrace connector in Claude and add it again with the same address.
The card looks out of date or a button is missingStart a new chat. If that doesn't help, disconnect and connect again so Claude loads the current card.
The wrong organization's projects appearDisconnect, connect again and pick the right organization on the approval page.
Today is empty or a warning says sources are behindChoose Open Sources in the card, or open Sources in Ondatrace, and reconnect the source that needs attention.
A send or meeting failsThe card shows what went wrong. Check that the email, chat or meeting account is still connected in Ondatrace, then try again.
Something elseAsk Claude to report an issue to Ondatrace support, use the help button in the card, or email ondatrace@ondatrace.com.

Disconnect

In Ondatrace, open Connected assistants and choose Disconnect. Access stops at once. You can also remove the connector in Claude's settings.

Your data

  • Claude receives only the Ondatrace records your account can see, when you or Claude ask for them.
  • Text copied from your emails, chats, tickets and meetings is marked as source material, and Claude is told to treat it as information, not instructions.
  • Ondatrace doesn't read your Claude conversations, memory or files. It receives only what a tool call sends.
  • Ondatrace keeps a record of each request Claude makes: who made it, which action and when.
