For templates bought from ondatrace.com. Questions: ondatrace@ondatrace.com.

Who can use it

One purchase covers everyone at the buyer's organization. Your order confirmation and the Start Here tab of your workbook show who bought it.

Consultants

If you are an independent consultant, your own business counts as the organization. You can use the templates on client projects and give clients the documents you produce. Your client doesn't get a license to reuse the blank template. If client staff need to work in the workbook themselves, their organization needs its own copy.

What you can do

  • Use the templates on as many of your organization's projects as you like.
  • Change them to suit how you work.
  • Share the project documents you produce with them, inside or outside your organization. A completed plan, report or register is yours to share.
  • Keep backup copies.

What you can't do

  • Sell, give away, share, upload or publish the templates or the examples, whether blank, completed or changed, in any form someone outside your organization could reuse as a template.
  • Include them in a product, course or template pack that you sell or give to others.
  • List them on a marketplace or file-sharing site.
  • Remove the copyright notice or the license details from the Start Here tab or the footers.
  • Use the questions, examples or response library to build a competing template product, or to train an AI model.

Updates and support

You get every update free for 12 months, including new major versions, sent to the email on your order.

Support is by email at ondatrace@ondatrace.com. We reply within two working days. Support covers getting the templates working in a supported version of Excel and answering questions about how they work. It doesn't cover building your project plan for you.

If the license ends

Your license ends if you break these terms or if your purchase is refunded. When it ends, stop using the templates and delete your copies. You can keep the project documents you already produced.
