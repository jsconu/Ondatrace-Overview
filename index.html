How it works

1. Connect

Plug in the tools you already have

Project tools, email, chat, calendars and meetings. It finds the projects in them, even ones nobody set up.

2. Connect the dots

It links projects, people and news

It matches the same work across tools, and outside changes to the projects they affect. You confirm the links.

3. Decide

You get the few things that need you

Each says why it matters, with a next step, the people side and a coaching tip.

Works with the tools you already use. Built on MCP, the open standard for connecting AI to business tools.

How our project management agent works

Harness the power of our specially designed project management AI agent. It does the reading and the writing, fixed rules make the calls that have to come out the same every time, and a person approves anything with real consequences.

Reads

Turns messy messages into facts

It reads emails, chat, tickets and meeting transcripts, pulls out the commitments, dates and decisions in them, and matches the same work across tools. Each fact keeps a link to the message it came from.

Judges

Rules decide, the agent explains

Project health and what needs attention come from fixed rules built with practicing project managers. The agent writes the explanation, the next step and the coaching tip, but it can't change the result.

Acts

You approve anything that matters

Saving evidence happens on its own, and so can small internal task updates if you allow it. Moving a committed milestone, closing a risk, sending a message or changing a record in your own tools always waits for a person.

Runs on its own

Works between your meetings

It checks your connected tools every 15 to 30 minutes, and within about a minute of a Slack message. It reruns the analysis only on projects where something changed, so a quiet project doesn't use AI time or send you anything new.

Stays in its lane

Permissions live in the app, not the agent

What each person can see is enforced by the application, not by instructions to the agent. If a finding rests on someone's private email, people who can't read that email don't see the finding at all.

Works with your AI

Your assistant can ask it too

A read-only MCP connection lets assistants like Claude or Copilot ask Ondatrace what needs attention and see the evidence, with the same permissions you have. Next: hosted access with per-person sign-in, then letting assistants propose actions for you to approve.

Ondatrace runs on Claude, from Anthropic. Text from emails, chats and tickets is treated as information to weigh, never as instructions to follow.

Why I built this

After years leading programs across teams, I've found the hardest project problems rarely live in the project plan. They're scattered across conversations, customer commitments, engineering work and decisions. Ondatrace is my bet that AI can pull that picture together without another system of record. It's a working prototype, and I'd love your feedback.
