AI Consulting for PMOs

Make AI work for your whole PMO, not just individual project managers.

AI can help your team move faster. But when every project manager builds separate agents and workflows, you can end up with duplicated effort, inconsistent outputs and fragmented oversight. I help PMO leaders choose high-value use cases, establish common standards and put AI to work across the portfolio.

Who it's for

For PMO leaders who want a shared AI approach instead of a patchwork of personal prompts, bots and agents. You don't need a dedicated AI team to start; you need clear priorities, ownership and guardrails.

What I help with

  • Identify repeatable, high-value use cases for status reporting, risk tracking, meeting follow-up and portfolio reviews
  • Spot dependencies, conflicting updates and emerging issues across projects instead of reviewing each project in isolation
  • Set shared rules for data access, human review, quality checks and responsible AI use
  • Decide when to use existing tools like Copilot, ChatGPT and Claude, and when a shared PMO workflow or agent is worth building
  • Reduce agent sprawl with common workflows, reusable practices and clear ownership across the PMO
