Privacy notice: website and template store

For templates bought from ondatrace.com. Questions: ondatrace@ondatrace.com.

Ondatrace LLC runs ondatrace.com and the template store. This notice covers the site and template purchases. The Ondatrace app (app.ondatrace.com) and its pilot have their own notice.

The short version

  • The templates run on your computer. We never receive the project information you put into them.
  • To sell and deliver templates we need your name, your email and your order details.
  • Card payments go to Stripe, our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • We email you about your order. We send marketing emails only if you opt in.
  • The website has no analytics and sets no tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell your information.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the store, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe: payment and checkout
  • Google Workspace: our email, which we use to send your files and answer you

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Marketing: opt out at any time with the link in any marketing email. Order emails aren't marketing.
  • Access, correction and deletion: email us. We'll delete what we can, except records the law requires us to keep.
  • Questions or complaints: ondatrace@ondatrace.com

Changes

We'll update this notice when how we handle information changes, and show the date below.
